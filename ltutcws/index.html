TOPIC 14: ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE



What is AI?
AI is a program that can "think" and "learn" from vast amounts 
of data. Unlike a regular program that only does exactly 
what it's told, AI can predict, create, and solve problems 
using natural language (like you are talking to a person).

Common AI Tools:
* Generative AI: Tools like Gemini or ChatGPT that write 
  essays, emails, or even computer code.
* Image Creators: Programs that turn "text descriptions" 
  into full digital paintings.
* Voice Assistants: Siri, Alexa, and Google Assistant.

How to use it safely:
1. Verify Facts: AI can sometimes "hallucinate" (make 
   up facts that sound true). Always double-check.
2. Privacy: Do not share personal passwords or private 
   bank details with an AI.
3. Prompts: The better your "prompt" (instruction), 
   the better the AI's answer will be.
