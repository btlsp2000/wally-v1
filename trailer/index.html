Trailer-Mini: The Original

Stepping Stone to Full-Scale Fabrication
This project serves as the geometric and structural bridge between conceptual drafting and full-scale trailer construction. It leverages the radial math of the WallyCAD engine to solve for articulated expansion and aerodynamic efficiency.

Project Assets & Links

WallyCAD Construction Documents Complete .dwg and coordinate data files for the radial chassis and frame intersections. Articulated Expansion Schematic (Side View) Geometric folding logic for the tessellated roof system and hinge placement. 1/4 Scale Model - Fabrication Log Documentation of structural integrity tests conducted at the woodshop. Bill of Materials: Reclaimed & Industrial Inventory of steel, hardware, and reclaimed materials for the prototype build.

Technical Specs

Geometry Engine: WallyCAD v1.0.10
Chassis Layout: Concentric Radial Sweep
Expansion Type: Articulated Faceted Top (Collapsible)
Status: Prototype/Stepping Stone Phase
